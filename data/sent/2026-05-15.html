Homemade Beef & Lamb Gyros Hack

A parchment-paper rolling trick that gets you spit-roast gyro texture from a home oven.

Prep: 30 min · Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 0.5 lb chuck steak, ground
  • 0.5 lb lamb, ground
  • 1 small onion, grated
  • 1 tbsp dried Greek oregano
  • 1 tsp garlic
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Greek olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove, grated (tzatziki)
  • 3 tbsp red wine vinegar (tzatziki)
  • Zest of 1 lemon (tzatziki)
  • 1 English cucumber, seeded and grated (tzatziki)
  • 1.5 tsp dried oregano (tzatziki)
  • 16 oz full-fat Greek yogurt (tzatziki)
  • Fresh dill to taste (tzatziki)
  • Warmed pita, caramelized onion, tomatoes, Greek feta, parsley to serve

Instructions

  1. Grind chuck and lamb together. Mix with grated onion, oregano, garlic, paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper.
  2. Place the mixture on parchment, cover with another sheet, and roll to ¼-inch thick.
  3. Drizzle with Greek olive oil and bake at 400°F for 20–25 minutes. Rest 8 minutes before slicing.
  4. For the tzatziki: combine grated garlic, red wine vinegar, lemon zest, squeezed cucumber, oregano, salt, pepper, Greek yogurt, and dill. Chill until needed.
  5. Assemble in warmed pita with meat, caramelized onion, tomato, feta, parsley, and a drizzle of olive oil.

Lazy Dan Dan Noodles in 15 Minutes

A one-pot dan dan with crispy pork and a tahini-chili sauce that comes together faster than delivery.

Prep: under 15 min

Ingredients

  • Noodles (quantity as desired)
  • Ground pork
  • Soy sauce (for pork)
  • Garlic
  • Tahini
  • Chili oil
  • Soy sauce (for sauce)
  • Sesame oil
  • Chinkiang vinegar
  • Sugar
  • Green onions

Instructions

  1. Cook noodles according to package instructions and set aside.
  2. Crisp the ground pork in a pan, then add soy sauce and garlic. Set aside.
  3. In the same pan, fry tahini, chili oil, soy sauce, sesame oil, Chinkiang vinegar, sugar, and green onions until combined.
  4. Toss the noodles and pork into the sauce until coated.
  5. Serve immediately.

Overnight Greek Yogurt Cream Cheese

Three ingredients, one sieve, and you've replaced the tub in your fridge.

Prep: 5 min active, overnight passive · Serves: 4–6

Ingredients

  • 500g 5% organic Greek yogurt
  • 1¼ tsp salt
  • 2 tsp lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Mix the salt and lemon juice into the Greek yogurt.
  2. Line a sieve with kitchen paper and set it over a bowl.
  3. Pour the yogurt mixture onto the paper.
  4. Leave in the fridge overnight to strain.
  5. Remove, shape as desired, and top with honey, olive oil, or fresh herbs.

Chemical Engineer's Science-Backed Lemon Loaf

A chemical engineer reverse-engineers maximum citrus intensity using flavor-molecule chemistry.

Prep: 20 min + 55–60 min bake · Serves: 1 loaf (8x4 pan)

Ingredients

  • 165g granulated sugar
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • Zest of 1 lime
  • Zest of 1 orange
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 egg
  • 75g olive oil
  • 65g almond milk
  • 145g AP flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 30g lemon juice (about ½ lemon)
  • 10g lime juice (about ½ lime)
  • 30g lemon juice (syrup)
  • 15g lime juice (syrup)
  • 25g powdered sugar (syrup)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 160°C/320°F (convection) or 180°C/350°F (no fan). Grease and line an 8x4 loaf tin.
  2. Combine sugar, all zests, and ginger in a large bowl. Rub together with your fingers until the sugar resembles damp yellow sand. Rest 15 minutes.
  3. Add egg and olive oil; whisk and let sit 5 minutes until most of the sugar dissolves.
  4. Add milk and whisk until fully dissolved.
  5. Add flour, baking powder, salt, lemon juice, and lime juice all at once; mix until just combined.
  6. Pour into the tin and bake 55–60 minutes, until golden and springy on top.
  7. Stir the syrup ingredients together. Rest the loaf 10 minutes out of the oven, then poke holes throughout with a skewer.
  8. Spoon the lemon-lime syrup over the hot loaf, including the edges, and let it soak in.
  9. Optional: glaze with runny Greek yogurt, powdered sugar, and lemon juice once nearly cool. Wrap tightly overnight for maximum moisture.
