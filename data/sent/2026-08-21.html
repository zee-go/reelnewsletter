Honey Baked Camembert with Peaches & Prosciutto

A molten wheel of camembert with peaches, prosciutto, walnuts, and honey — the entire appetizer course in one dish.

Prep: 25 min (including resting time) · Serves: 2–4

Ingredients

  • 1 wheel camembert
  • 2–3 peaches, sliced
  • 3–4 slices prosciutto (jambon cru)
  • Handful of walnuts
  • Drizzle of honey

Instructions

  1. Remove camembert from the fridge and let it rest at room temperature for 20 minutes.
  2. Slice peaches into pieces.
  3. Place camembert in a baking dish and top with peach slices, prosciutto, walnuts, and a drizzle of honey.
  4. Bake at 190°C / 380°F for 15–20 minutes until the cheese is fully melted and golden.
  5. Serve immediately with bread or crackers.

Summer Market Charcuterie Dip

If the camembert felt too tidy, here's a messier baked Brie with blackberries, peaches, and hot garlic honey.

Prep: 15–20 min · Serves: 2–4

Ingredients

  • 8 oz Brie, cut into large cubes
  • 1/4 cup fresh blackberries
  • 1/2 medium peach, diced
  • 2 oz prosciutto, torn into pieces
  • 1/4 cup Marcona almonds, roughly chopped
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
  • 1/2 cup hot garlic honey

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 375°F.
  2. Add Brie, blackberries, peaches, and prosciutto to a small baking dish.
  3. Season lightly with thyme, salt, and black pepper.
  4. Drizzle with hot garlic honey and bake for 12–15 minutes, until Brie is soft and gooey and fruit is bubbling.
  5. Finish with Marcona almonds, more fresh thyme, and an extra drizzle of hot garlic honey.
  6. Serve immediately with toasted bread, crostini, or crackers.
