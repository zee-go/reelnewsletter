Food

Mediterranean Eggplant Pocket Bake

Roasted eggplant slices folded into little pockets around spiced beef, baked in marinara under bubbly mozzarella.

Prep: ~45 min total · Serves: 6–8 pockets

Ingredients

  • 2–3 medium eggplants, sliced 1/4–1/3 inch thick
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • 1 lb 80/20 ground beef
  • 1/3 yellow onion, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 tbsp onion powder
  • 1 tbsp granulated garlic
  • 1/2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley
  • Salt & pepper
  • 1/2 cup uncooked rice (optional)
  • 1/2 jar marinara or pasta sauce
  • Sliced or shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Chili flakes
  • Fresh basil
  • Shredded parmesan

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Slice eggplants 1/4–1/3 inch thick, lay on tray without overlapping, season with salt and drizzle with olive oil.
  3. Roast eggplant for 18–20 minutes.
  4. Mix ground beef with diced onion, garlic, spices, salt, pepper, and optional uncooked rice.
  5. Form mixture into 6–8 equal patties.
  6. Let eggplant cool 1 min, then cross two slices in an X on your palm, place a patty in center, fold sides over to form a pocket.
  7. Spread half a jar of marinara in an oven-safe dish, place pockets on top, cover with lid, and bake at 425°F for 22–24 minutes.
  8. Remove lid, top with mozzarella and chili flakes, broil 2–3 minutes until melted and bubbly.
  9. Serve topped with parmesan and fresh basil, optionally alongside rice.

5-Day Dolomites Road Trip Itinerary

A day-by-day plan covering the Dolomites' best hikes, viewpoints, and the case for renting a van.

  • Days 1–3 hit the iconic hikes: Cadini di Misurina, Tre Cime di Lavaredo, Lago di Braies, and the tougher 7.5-mile Lago di Sorapis trail.
  • Days 4–5 cover the scenic side: Seceda cable car, Church of St John, St Magdalena viewpoint, and Alpe di Siusi.
  • A van (Roadsurfer out of Venice works) beats pricey hotels — campgrounds run about €50/night per group.
  • Most campgrounds don't take reservations, so roll in by early afternoon to lock in a spot.
