Stop Trying: Effortless Action and Belief

Perceived difficulty is mimetic — stop straining and let belief replace effortful thinking.

  • A panda grimaces breaking bamboo because it copied humans — nocebo and predictive processing inflate struggle.
  • Girard's mimetic theory: we imitate desires and actions from prestigious mediators, making neutral tasks feel hard.
  • Wu-wei, Stoicism, and Schopenhauer converge — the intellect interferes with instinct.
  • Belief isn't new information; it's forgetting the limiting concepts you picked up.

Food

Kefbab: Kebab, Tzatziki & Sweet Potato Wedges

Grilled ground beef kebab with homemade tzatziki and spiced sweet potato wedges.

Prep: 20 min

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Paprika
  • Chili
  • Cumin
  • Coriander
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Parsley
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Greek yogurt
  • Cucumber
  • Dill
  • Lemon juice
  • Olive oil
  • Honey
  • Sweet potatoes
  • Olive oil (for wedges)
  • Chili flakes

Instructions

  1. Mix ground beef with paprika, chili, cumin, coriander, garlic, onion, parsley, salt, and pepper.
  2. Press the mixture flat on a tray and pre-cut into portions.
  3. Grill on max heat with the tray very close to the element until crispy outside and juicy inside.
  4. For tzatziki, mix Greek yogurt, cucumber, dill, parsley, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, salt, pepper, and a little honey.
  5. Cut sweet potatoes into wedges and toss with olive oil, paprika, cumin, coriander, chili flakes, salt, pepper, and honey.
  6. Bake the wedges at 250°C for 20–30 minutes.

Greek Lamb Chops (Paidakia)

Juicy grilled lamb chops marinated in olive oil, lemon, garlic, and oregano.

Prep: 2 hrs 30 min (including marinating) · Serves: 2–3

Ingredients

  • 8 lamb chops
  • 50ml extra virgin Greek olive oil
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated
  • 2 tbsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1½ tsp pink Himalayan sea salt
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, thyme, salt, and pepper into a marinade.
  2. Coat the lamb chops, cover, and refrigerate for at least 2 hours.
  3. Pull from the fridge and rest at room temperature for 20–30 minutes.
  4. Heat a griddle pan over medium-high for 3–4 minutes, then cook 4 minutes per side plus 30 seconds fat-side down.
  5. Rest the chops on a plate for at least 5 minutes.
  6. Pour the leftover marinade into a saucepan with a splash of water, boil briskly for 3–4 minutes, then spoon over the chops. Serve with lemon, tzatziki, and potato salad.
