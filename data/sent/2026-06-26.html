Why ADHD Women Crash Before Their Period

Estrogen runs dopamine, so the luteal-phase drop hits ADHD brains twice as hard.

  • Almost 50% of women with ADHD report PMDD, versus 28% in the general population.
  • ADHD is a dopamine-regulation difference, and estrogen directly drives dopamine.
  • When estrogen falls, already-low dopamine collapses — taking executive function with it.
  • Many were medicated for anxiety or depression for years before the cycle link surfaced.
