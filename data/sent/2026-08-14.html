High-Protein Pumpkin Chocolate Brownie

A low-calorie, high-protein pumpkin brownie made in a blender with egg whites and cocoa powder.

Prep: 45 min · Serves: 4 pieces

Ingredients

  • 250g baked pumpkin (or butternut squash)
  • 125ml egg whites (4 egg whites)
  • 17.5g cocoa powder
  • 12.5g oat flour
  • 25g chocolate chips (70% dark)
  • 30–40g sweetener (stevia or monk fruit)
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • ¼ tsp baking powder
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Roast pumpkin cut-side down at 400°F / 200°C for ~35 minutes until soft. Let cool and remove skin.
  2. Add pumpkin, egg whites, cocoa powder, oat flour, sweetener, vanilla, baking powder, and salt to a blender. Blend until smooth.
  3. Pour batter into a baking dish, sprinkle chocolate chips on top, and bake until set (center should be slightly jiggly).
  4. Let cool, then refrigerate. Serve cold, optionally on top of yogurt.

Crispy Feta Phyllo Bites with Honey

Easy buttery phyllo appetizers filled with feta, fig preserves, and pistachios, drizzled with honey.

Ingredients

  • Phyllo dough
  • Butter (for layering and baking)
  • Feta cheese
  • Fig preserves
  • Chives
  • Spices (to taste)
  • Pistachios
  • Honey (for drizzle)

Instructions

  1. Layer phyllo sheets with melted butter between each layer.
  2. Add feta, fig preserves, chives, spices, and pistachios as filling.
  3. Roll the phyllo tightly around the filling.
  4. Cut into pieces with a sharp knife.
  5. Brush with butter and bake until nice and golden.
  6. Drizzle with honey and serve.
