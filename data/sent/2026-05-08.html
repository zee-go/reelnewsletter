Marketing

You Are the Niche: Personal Brand Strategy

Stop picking a niche — build the brand around who you actually are.

  • Lead with an anchor topic, a personal journey, or both — the combination is where loyalty lives.
  • People connect to humans with perspective, not to content categories.
  • Your real brand is whatever you think about daily and could talk about for hours without notes.
  • Mixing expertise with a personal narrative (like a public growth bet) adds the entertainment layer that pulls audiences deeper.

Psychology

Co-Regulation: Why Your Circle Shapes Your Nervous System

Your brain mirrors the people around you, so stressed company keeps your system on edge.

  • The fusiform gyrus reads faces and body language, tuning your nervous system to match.
  • Time around dysregulated people keeps you locked in low-grade threat detection.
  • Saying no conserves internal resources — burnout is borrowing from tomorrow to pay for today.
  • Boundaries (or full cutoffs) with draining people count as legitimate regulation, not cruelty.

Stop Letting Fear Write the Full Story

Mental discipline is refusing to treat the worst-case scenario as the only case.

  • Fixating on one bad outcome makes the body react as if it's already happening.
  • Widening the frame to multiple possible outcomes physically calms the system.
  • Thoughts are interpretations, not facts — and interpretations are editable.
  • Discipline isn't forced positivity; it's holding neutral so negativity doesn't become the default lens.

by @The Flint · Watch →

Stop Fixing ADHD — Start Power-Maxing It

ADHD is an interest-based nervous system; it gets powerful when you stop fighting it.

  • Focus is activated by genuine interest, not willpower — design for engagement instead of forcing it.
  • Crashes aren't laziness; they're the tax on overmasking and running a neurodivergent brain on neurotypical settings.
  • Close small open loops, allow side quests, and rest before the crash, not after.
  • Trade "perfect" for "done" and build life around what actually engages you.

by @We Who War Within · Watch →

Hack Your ADHD With Parasympathetic Routines

Bookend the day with calming routines and hyperfixation becomes something you can switch on.

  • Alcohol's appeal to ADHD brains hints they're chasing a parasympathetic, down-regulated state.
  • Parasympathetic routines at the start and end of the day stabilize dopamine and set the tone.
  • High-stimulation behaviors used strategically mid-day can trigger productive hyperfixation.
  • The real superpower is learning to enter and exit hyperfixation on demand.
