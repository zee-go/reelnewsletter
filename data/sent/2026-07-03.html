Baked Peaches & Brie Charcuterie Dip

A summer charcuterie board baked into one bubbly, scoopable dip with peaches, Brie, and hot honey.

Prep: 5 min

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe peaches, diced
  • 1/2 cup blackberries
  • 8 oz Brie, chopped
  • 2 oz prosciutto, torn
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
  • Kosher salt & black pepper to taste
  • Aleppo hot honey (to taste)
  • Balsamic drizzle (for finishing)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  2. Add peaches, blackberries, Brie, prosciutto, and thyme to a baking dish.
  3. Pour Aleppo hot honey over the top and sprinkle with salt and pepper.
  4. Bake for 12–15 minutes until the Brie is melted and bubbling.
  5. Remove from oven and drizzle with balsamic.
  6. Serve warm with garlic-rubbed crostini or crackers.

Greek Sheet Pan Chicken with Ladolemono

Juicy chicken thighs roasted with veggies, olives, and feta in a lemon-garlic sauce — dinner and dishes handled in one move.

Prep: 10 min

Ingredients

  • Fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2–3 cloves garlic
  • Kosher salt
  • Black pepper
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 1 zucchini, sliced
  • 1 orange bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • Boneless, skinless chicken thighs
  • Kalamata olives
  • Castelvetrano olives
  • Feta cheese, crumbled
  • Fresh Italian parsley

Instructions

  1. Slice the red onion, zucchini, orange bell pepper, and tomato.
  2. Spread the veggies across a sheet pan.
  3. Whisk together lemon juice, oregano, garlic, salt, pepper, and olive oil to make the Ladolemono sauce.
  4. Drizzle the sauce over the veggies and chicken thighs, tossing to coat.
  5. Nestle the seasoned chicken thighs between the veggies on the pan.
  6. Bake at 425°F for about 35 minutes, or until the chicken is fully cooked through.
  7. Top with olives, crumbled feta, and fresh parsley before serving.
