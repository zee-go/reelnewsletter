AI Tools Lower the Barrier to Digital Creation

6 picks · AI & Marketing first · Read online →

This week leaned heavy on AI workflows — agents that brief you, build for you, and film for you. We also picked up a personal brand roadmap, a small-wins mindset, and a skincare shortcut on the side.

AI

Claude Code Automates Full Video Production

A hands-off pipeline that turns one terminal command into finished UGC video.

  • Claude Code connects to Higgsfield through Playwright MCP, taking full control of the browser to navigate and submit prompts.
  • Custom Claude skills are tuned for Seedance 2.0 — model selection, camera motion, and final output all handled.
  • Trigger the entire workflow with a single sentence in your terminal; Claude runs the rest.
  • Pitched as a way to replace a marketing team for product UGC at scale.

3D Websites From Videos, No Coding Needed

Emergent's E1 agent plus Claude turns an AI video into an interactive parallax site.

  • Connect your GitHub to Emergent, pick the E1 agent, and feed it a prompt file with your video and an inspiration link.
  • Use any AI video generator to spin up a 3D-style video from a single photo as your starting asset.
  • Emergent's Plot handles the build, outputting a customizable parallax site automatically.
  • Easy to package as a productized service — no coding or 3D chops required.

by @Jo Mendes · Watch →

Build a Daily Briefing Agent with Claude

Claude Managed Agents lets you spin up autonomous agents in plain English.

  • Describe the task in natural language; Claude builds and runs the agent for you.
  • One-click connections to Gmail, Google Calendar, and web search power a 7am Slack brief.
  • The agent runs overnight, surfacing top news, key emails, and calendar events before you're up.
  • Same pattern extends to support agents, ops runners, and marketing reports.

Marketing

Build a Personal Brand in 3.5 Months

A week-by-week roadmap to launch, grow, and monetize a personal brand.

  • Weeks 1–2: mind-map your life, interests, and experiences — you are the niche, not a topic.
  • Month 1: post 5–7x/week, turn one idea into five, add personal "mirror" content, then review watch time, saves, and shares.
  • Month 2: amplify what landed, post discovery + connection + trust content weekly, cycle-sync creation if applicable.
  • Month 3: batch-film a buffer and layer in money content — mirror, magnet, money every week.

Psychology

Delusional Optimism: Win Small, Dream Big

Stack tiny wins until the audacious dream feels inevitable.

  • Reframe big dreams as easy by breaking them into small, actionable tasks.
  • Start each morning with a "winning streak" — making your bed counts, and the momentum is real.
  • Small wins build optimism, optimism breeds delusion, delusion fuels fearless action.
  • Expect resistance: chasing big dreams makes you a target for doubt — from others and from yourself.
