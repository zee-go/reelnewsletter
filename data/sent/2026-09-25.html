Food

Killer Crack Chicken Marinade

A punchy, high-heat marinade that turns weeknight chicken into something worth remembering.

Ingredients

  • olive oil
  • sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • honey
  • lemon juice
  • garlic
  • ginger

Instructions

  1. Whisk together olive oil, sesame oil, soy sauce, Worcestershire, honey, lemon juice, garlic, and ginger in a bowl.
  2. Add chicken and marinate — the longer, the better.
  3. Bake at 450°F for 35 minutes.

Spicy Short Rib Brothy Rice Bowl

Pressure-cooked short ribs swimming in coconut red curry broth over rice — comfort food with real depth.

Prep: 45 min pressure cook + prep

Ingredients

  • short ribs
  • salt
  • red curry paste
  • shallots
  • ginger
  • garlic
  • soy sauce
  • beef stock
  • fish sauce
  • brown sugar
  • coconut milk
  • rice
  • green onions
  • sesame seeds

Instructions

  1. Season short ribs with salt and sear on all sides in the pressure cooker.
  2. Fry red curry paste in the pot, then add shallots, ginger, and garlic.
  3. Add ribs back in with soy sauce, beef stock, fish sauce, and brown sugar.
  4. Pressure cook on high for 45 minutes, then let pressure release naturally.
  5. Shred meat from the short ribs.
  6. Strain aromatics from the broth and stir in coconut milk.
  7. Serve hot broth over rice and shredded beef; garnish with green onions and sesame seeds.
