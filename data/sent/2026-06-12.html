Food

Mother-in-Law's Easy Preserved Lemons

A low-effort jar that pays you back for weeks in salads, grains, and roasted veg.

Prep: 15 min · Serves: 1 jar

Ingredients

  • 1–4 lemons (depending on jar size)
  • Coarse sea salt, several generous pinches
  • Olive oil, enough to fully cover the lemons

Instructions

  1. Sterilize a clean glass jar with boiling water and let it dry.
  2. Wash the lemons and slice into thin rounds.
  3. Layer lemon slices and a good pinch of salt alternately until the jar is packed.
  4. Press everything down firmly so the lemons start releasing their juice.
  5. Pour olive oil over the top until the lemons are fully submerged.
  6. Seal and store in a cool place for at least 3 days before using — 1–2 weeks gets you that mellow, fermented depth.

Greek Chicken Thighs Sheet Pan Dinner

One pan, yogurt-marinated thighs, and a Mediterranean cast of supporting players.

Ingredients

  • Chicken thighs
  • Greek yogurt (for the marinade)
  • Lemon (for the marinade)
  • Potatoes
  • Butter beans
  • Artichokes
  • Olives

Instructions

  1. Whisk together the Greek yogurt and lemon marinade and coat the chicken thighs thoroughly.
  2. Arrange the marinated thighs on a sheet pan.
  3. Scatter the potatoes, butter beans, artichokes, and olives around the chicken.
  4. Bake until the chicken is cooked through and the potatoes are tender.
